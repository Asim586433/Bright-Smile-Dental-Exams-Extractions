Our Dental Services

We focus on providing specialized dental exams and extractions with the latest technology and a gentle approach.

Comprehensive Dental Exams

Thorough examinations to assess your oral health, including digital X-rays, oral cancer screening, and gum disease evaluation. We create personalized treatment plans based on your needs.

  • Digital X-rays
  • Oral Cancer Screening
  • Gum Health Assessment

Gentle Tooth Extractions

Safe and comfortable tooth removal procedures for damaged, decayed, or problematic teeth. We use advanced techniques to minimize discomfort and promote quick healing.

  • Simple Extractions
  • Wisdom Teeth Removal
  • Post-Extraction Care
